How we work

No cart and no online payment: a specialist reviews every inquiry.

  1. Send the code or the duty

    The code on the part, a photo of its markings, or the dimensions and operating conditions, by WhatsApp or the form.

  2. Technical match

    A specialist matches the code and checks equivalents where needed.

  3. Availability and terms

    Availability, lead time and commercial terms are confirmed directly.

  4. Supply

    Once agreed, the part is supplied.
